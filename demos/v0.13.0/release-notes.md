# Follow the evidence — v0.13.0 feature demo

[Watch or download the narrated 1080p demo](https://github.com/Scoston/ai-assisted-ocsf-incident-timeline/releases/download/demo-v0.13.0/timeline-v0.13.0-demo.mp4).

Approximately eight minutes with 20 chapters, burned captions, an optional English subtitle track, and a complete transcript. Covers the real viewer, evidence verification, 19 collector types, GitHub/Kubernetes audit sources, native Plaso coverage, OCSF export, signatures, Tines, Databricks, ten Jupyter notebooks, task-specific AI routes, token budgets/cache, recovery, shared access, and validation.

All evidence is synthetic. Cloud integration sections are configuration walkthroughs; the AI cache and collector recovery sections identify their simulated providers. Native fixture totals and benchmarks refer to recorded release validation. Narration uses the stock Kokoro `af_sarah` voice. No paid model calls or live tenant connections were made.

[Chapter guide and commands](https://github.com/Scoston/ai-assisted-ocsf-incident-timeline/blob/main/docs/DEMO.md) · [Production recipe and attribution](https://github.com/Scoston/ai-assisted-ocsf-incident-timeline/blob/main/demos/v0.13.0/README.md)

Download the `.srt` or `.vtt` for separate captions, `-transcript.md` for the full narration, `-chapters.txt` for timestamps, `-provenance.json` for the source/build identifiers, and `SHA256SUMS` to verify the media. The source-capture ZIP and commit-named candidate retain the production evidence used to review the final files.
